# Why this exists

Hi my name is Chris. I started working with ai in February of this year and I wanted to try and make myself a living while learning. I started off being broken in identity, health, and finances. The idea for Ragnarok was small. It was originally called a POS. A personal operating system. There were three branches. MoneyTalks, Daily ops, and IWantToBeHappyAgain. It was about me rebuilding myself while trying to learn from my mistakes. I wanted to make something that I could be remembered by and that I can hand to my kids when they got older. It started off small selling personal items, telling myself I can be a better person for my family, trying to take life a small step at a time. It became a small scale iPhone flipping operation based off of an air table that I created with ai. I didnt look up any frameworks or how other people ran their shit. I didn’t want it to mess with my own vision. I learned about how some of the ai function works from the ground up just by asking the ai I was working with questions. It blew up into some grand idea that the grass was greener on the other side and that I wanted to own the whole ecosystem. I began building my foundation being wanting to find truth in what I lost and what I did to deserve it. I started coding with ai in around April. I realized I’m not who I set out to be and I’m still nowhere close. These repos are what I built wanting to become a solodev/founder. There’s a lot of omitted story since I dont really have the attention span nor the drive to write it all out. I hope they help with anyone’s project and hope that it helps build that ecosystem that I longed for. Life is what you make it. Getting from a->c while living in the present is the hardest and probably the best and the worst part of living life. Provable is only something you can find yourself. Probable is what you tell yourself when you’re trying to find it. Good luck with your own Ragnarok. I’m trying to make it out of mine.
